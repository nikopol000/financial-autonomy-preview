# WEB premium benchmark — 2026-10-27 (research, not a financial contract)

## Scope and evidence limits
Public documentation and a small number of first-person community comments were reviewed on 27 Oct 2026. This is a **qualitative, non-representative** sample, not usability testing of Financial Autonomy, a comparative feature certification, or evidence of users' universal preferences. No competitor layout, code, copy, or formulas are copied. The product remains a **tool**, not a coach.

## Observations and transferable principles

1. **YNAB: targets, allocated cash and account semantics are distinct.** Its 2026 getting-started glossary separates actual cash available to assign, category targets, and credit-card payment categories; transfer handling is explicit. This supports *clear labels for the provenance of a number*, not adopting its zero-based budgeting model. Financial Autonomy's planned budget must remain separate from owned-money availability and card credit. Source: https://support.ynab.com/en_us/getting-started-ynab-basics-and-key-terms-BJCaIHaTZe (retrieved 2026-10-27).
2. **Actual Budget: local-first speed and data custody.** Its product overview stresses speed and user control; its installation documentation warns that browser-only storage without a backup can be lost and recommends backing up after sessions. Actual also supports optional sync, which Financial Autonomy must **not** introduce. Transferable principle: make encrypted-backup availability and vault recovery limitations understandable, with no false durability guarantee. Sources: https://actualbudget.org/ and https://master.www.actualbudget.org/docs/install/ (retrieved 2026-10-27).
3. **Community feedback: pay-cycle flexibility.** A user in r/selfhosted asked specifically for budgeting periods matching pay frequency, including four-week and last-working-day cycles. This is one request, **not** population-level evidence. Financial Autonomy already supports multiple windows; explain them in ordinary language before adding more modes. Source: https://www.reddit.com/r/selfhosted/comments/19ci5wv/feedback_on_personal_finance_wallet_app/ (2024 thread; retrieved 2026-10-27).
4. **Community feedback: setup friction and variable income.** A first-person r/personalfinance report describes multiple failed attempts to understand a budgeting product with irregular income; replies disagree about the cause. This is anecdotal and old, not a verdict on YNAB or Financial Autonomy. Transferable principle: minimize cross-screen hops for the first useful action; do not promise that a plan is spendable cash. Source: https://www.reddit.com/r/personalfinance/comments/7lok8z/any_point_in_ynab/ (2017 thread; retrieved 2026-10-27).

## Decision record for WEB

- **Implemented in 18.59 candidate/PR #175:** direct `Configurează primul buget` button in the empty Bugete screen opens the existing budget editor, without adding a duplicate editor, changing the budget engine, or interpreting credit as owned money. Synthetic Chromium 320/390 light/dark demo journey validates entry and persistence in the existing in-memory demo.
- **Next non-financial UX candidates:** compact demo banner and accessible navigation under 200% zoom (#85), and a clear, non-destructive blocked-vault recovery explanation (#35). Validate on actual browser/iPhone where possible; a synthetic Chromium zoom proxy is not Safari certification.
- **#14 DISCOVERY ONLY:** three prospective views—planned budget, owned-money context, remaining-days context—must each identify source balances, included accounts, time horizon, treatment of transfers, negative balances, reserved obligations, future income, credit and unexplained differences. A displayed numeric zero must never silently mean “not calculable.” No modes or formula changes before explicit approved testable contract.
- **Example requiring a decision, not a proposed calculation:** a planned 1,500 RON monthly category, 900 RON checking, 400 RON savings (not yet selected), 3,000 RON unused credit and 700 RON upcoming obligations do **not** justify labeling 3,600 RON as spendable. Whether any of the 200 RON checking remainder may fund this category depends on account selection, other categories, timing and reserves. A later unexplained 200 RON checking reduction cannot be silently booked as an expense.
- **Safety boundary:** do not infer missing transactions from observed balances; do not auto-delete KEY-only ciphertext metadata or bypass old cached-tab reset hazards. Keep local-only, encrypted file backup, no backend, login, telemetry, bank connection or iOS changes.
